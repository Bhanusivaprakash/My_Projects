# My_Projects

This repository is a **living portfolio** of my work and experiments in **robotics, mechanical design, electronics design, and robotic simulation**. The projects here reflect a hands-on, engineering-first mindset — building things that *actually work*, not just look good on paper.

My core interest lies in **robotic systems** where **mechanical design, electronics, and software intersect**. Most projects are either:

* Design-heavy (CAD, manufacturability, kinematics)
* Electronics-focused (schematics, power, signal integrity)
* Simulation & control-oriented (ROS2, system behavior, testing before hardware)

### ⚙️ Mechanical Design

## Tools & Technologies

### 🧩 Mechanical CAD

I am comfortable working across multiple CAD ecosystems:

* **SolidWorks** – Primary tool for complex assemblies, mechanisms, and professional-grade designs
* **Solid Edge** – Strong experience with parametric modeling and assembly workflows
* **FreeCAD** – Open-source CAD for parametric and scripting-based design

Use cases:

* Mechanism design
* Assembly motion checks
* Export-ready models for manufacturing and 3D printing

---

### 🖥️ Electronics CAD & PCB Design

* **KiCad** – Main tool for schematic capture and PCB layout
* **EasyEDA** – Rapid prototyping and quick-turn PCB designs

Experience includes:

* Multi-layer boards
* Power and signal routing
* Footprint creation
* ERC/DRC-driven design discipline

---

### 🔍 Circuit Simulation

* **LTspice** – Used extensively for:

  * Analog circuit simulation
  * Power supply behavior
  * Transient and frequency-domain analysis

Simulation-first approach to avoid costly hardware mistakes.

---

### 🧠 Robotics Middleware & Simulation

* **ROS2 (Humble Hawksbill)**

Capabilities:

* Node-based architecture understanding
* Publisher–Subscriber model
* Basic robot description (URDF/XACRO)
* Simulation-oriented workflows
* Integration mindset for sensors, actuators, and controllers

ROS2 is used as the backbone for scalable and modular robotic systems.
